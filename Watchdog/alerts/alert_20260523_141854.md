2.  **Error:** Encountered `SecurityError` due to missing Keychain permissions.
3.  **Crash:** Exception was not caught gracefully, causing the cron process to exit with status 1.
4.  **Shutdown:** The unhandled failure triggered a fatal shutdown of the Hermes Agent Gateway process.
